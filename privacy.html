Privacy Policy

Orbly: Ball Sort Puzzle. Effective date: September 28, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Orbly ("the game") handles information. The game is developed by Mrcfaruk ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or location. The game does not require an account.

Your game progress and settings (current level and your sound, vibration and color-blind mode preferences) are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system and language
  • Information about how you interact with ads

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings, then Privacy settings, inside the game.
  • You can delete all game data stored on your device by uninstalling the game.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
